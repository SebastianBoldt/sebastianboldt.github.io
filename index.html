What I Do

Clean architecture, modular design, maintainability.

I build iOS apps with a focus on these principles.

Architecture & Modularity

I have worked with MVVM, VIPER and more traditional patterns. Today I mostly use The Composable Architecture (TCA) and the Point‑Free ecosystem, a Redux-style approach. With Swift Package Manager I draw clear boundaries between modules, so features stay reusable and easy to slice out. Whatever the pattern, I inject dependencies explicitly so the code stays testable and easy to change.

TCA SPM MVVM MVC VIPER Clean Architecture

Framework Knowledge

I work with SwiftUI every day and have a lot of UIKit experience from large projects. I have also worked with AudioKit, StoreKit, CloudKit, WidgetKit, MapKit, Core Data and the iOS Accessibility APIs. When a legacy codebase needs maintaining or extending, I work in Objective-C too.

Swift SwiftUI UIKit Objective-C

Team Collaboration & Mentoring

I have mentored developers and given talks on iOS topics. I have worked in large international teams with mono-repo and multi-repo setups, and in smaller local teams. I have automated builds and releases with Xcode Cloud, Bitrise and Jenkins.
